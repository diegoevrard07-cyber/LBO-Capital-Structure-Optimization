# Investment Committee Memo — Project Checkout (Tesco plc, TSCO.L)

**All figures in GBP millions from model outputs on the bundled fundamentals
snapshot (Yahoo Finance, captured 20 Aug 2026). Re-running live will move the
numbers; the logic will not move.**

## Why this is an LBO target

Tesco is the textbook profile: the UK's #1 grocer with defensive,
utility-like cash flows (people buy food in recessions), capex of only ~2% of
revenue, modest existing leverage, and an entry price of 8.9x EBITDA — low
enough that debt capacity, not price, is the binding question.

## Transaction

Acquisition at **9.9x EV/EBITDA** (8.9x market + 1.0x control premium) on
entry EBITDA of **£5,054m** — enterprise value **£50,287m**. Five-year hold,
exit underwritten at the entry multiple (no re-rating assumed).

| Uses | £m | Sources | £m |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Purchase price (EV) | 50,287 | Senior TLB (4.00x @ S+350, 1%/yr amort) | 20,216 |
| Transaction & financing fees | 1,355 | Second Lien (0.75x @ S+650, bullet) | 3,790 |
| | | Sponsor equity | 27,635 |

Plus a £2,527m undrawn revolver (0.5x, S+300, 50bp commitment fee) as the
liquidity backstop. Blended cash cost of debt **7.7%** at a 3.75% base rate.

## Optimized structure and returns

The optimizer scanned 231 leverage × senior-mix structures, requiring every
one to pass a 2.0x interest-coverage covenant and a stepping-down net-leverage
covenant in every year of the base case AND to survive a stressed downside
(−20% growth haircut, −10% year-1 EBITDA shock, −1.0x exit multiple) with the
equity intact.

**Recommendation: 4.75x total leverage, 100% senior-first mix** (4.00x TLB +
0.75x second lien):

- Base case: **IRR 10.5%, MOIC 1.65x** on the £27,635m cheque; exit equity
  £45,564m.
- Value creation of £17,928m: **£11,219m EBITDA growth + £8,064m debt
  paydown + £0 multiple expansion − £1,355m fees** — the deal earns its
  return; it does not bet on the exit.
- Credit: minimum interest coverage 2.82x (covenant 2.0x); peak net leverage
  4.32x, deleveraging to ~2.6x by exit on a 75% excess-cash-flow sweep.
- Stress case: remains covenant-compliant throughout; equity survives at
  1.06x MOIC (1.2% IRR) — capital preserved through a recession.
- Monte Carlo (5,000 draws on growth, exit multiple, rates): median IRR
  10.7%, P5/P95 2.6%/18.7%, **probability of distress 4.3%**, equity wipeout
  0.0%.

## Sensitivities

- Entry price is the dominant risk: 0.5x overpaid at entry costs ~2.5pp of
  IRR — more than a 0.5x exit de-rating (~1.8pp).
- Leverage amplifies the exit both ways: at a 8.0x de-rated exit, MORE debt
  produces a LOWER IRR — 6.0x leverage returns less than 2.0x.

## What the optimizer found that max-leverage misses

The naive max-IRR structure (6.00x) offers 11.2% — only 0.7pp more — and
breaches its interest-coverage covenant in year 1 of the stressed case
(1.84x vs the 2.00x minimum), handing the company to its lenders in the first
recession. **Sixty-six basis points of headline IRR is the entire price of a
structure that keeps the company; the optimizer pays it.**
